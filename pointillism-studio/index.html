How this works & what to expect

This studio models optical mixing: your eyes average light from separate dots. It averages colors in linear-light sRGB, then converts back for the screen. It does not predict physically mixed paint. Yellow and blue dots therefore do not automatically make the same green as mixed yellow and blue pigments.

Deconstruction is approximate and not unique. The finder searches sets of three different enabled markers in 5% steps, with at least 10% of each. It compares the resulting color—including your paper setting—to the target in OKLab. A small distance means a closer screen match, not a confidence percentage. Some targets cannot be reached with your palette.

Viewing distance smoothly reduces visible dot contrast toward the calculated average; it is an illustration, not a calibrated vision simulation. Real paint, lighting, paper, dot size and your display all affect the result. Test a small swatch before a larger piece.
